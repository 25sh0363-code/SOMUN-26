## Who it's for

SOMUN '26 is open to **school students from grade VIII to grade XII**. Whether it's your first conference or your fifteenth, you're welcome — chambers are balanced across experience levels, and first-timers get a full procedure briefing on Day 01 before the gavel ever falls.

You can join **as an individual delegate** (the secretariat matches you into balanced committees) or **with your school's delegation** — one form covers the whole group, and every member keeps their own reference code.

## How registration works

The portal walks you through four short pages:

1. **Personal information** — your details, school and emergency contact.
2. **MUN experience** — how many conferences you've done (none is a perfectly good answer) and any achievements.
3. **Committee preferences** — list three chambers, with an optional preferred portfolio. The allocation matrix on the form shows every chamber's available portfolios before you choose.
4. **Referral & payment** — came through a friend? Add their referral. Then pay the fee shown on screen via any UPI app.

Payment is simple: the site generates **your exact amount with your own reference**, you scan the QR or pay via any UPI app, submit the transaction ID and a screenshot of the success screen — and you instantly get your **reference code** and a confirmation. When the secretariat verifies your payment, your confirmation mail lands in your inbox.

Allotments are decided by the secretariat and arrive **by email** — then your committee's background guide waits for you in Resources, and your QR pass gets you through the doors on Day 01.

## The twelve chambers

DISEC · UNHRC · ECOSOC · UNODC · UNCTC · UNSCW · UNOOSA · HCC · ICC · AIPPM · MCU · IP (International Press)

Every chamber has its own dossier on the site — agendas, focus areas, and the portfolios open for that committee.

## What your pass covers

The delegate fee covers the delegate kit, all three days of meals, socials entry and certificates. Accommodation isn't provided — outstation delegates can write to the secretariat for hotel suggestions near the venue.

## Schools & delegations

Schools can register a delegation in one go — each delegate still gets a personal reference code, portfolio preferences and allotment. Delegation members are allocated together wherever chamber balance allows.

## Questions?

Everything from dress code to change-of-committee requests is answered in the **FAQs** page. Anything else, write to **somundelaffairs@gmail.com** — the secretariat responds from the registered email.
