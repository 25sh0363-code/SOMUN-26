01 The Conference

A chamber where
the world argues
toward peace.

SOMUN was founded on a simple conviction — that the students who learn to negotiate today will refuse to escalate tomorrow. Over three days each October, our committees replicate the pressure, procedure and persuasion of the real United Nations, guided by an executive board of champion delegates and former secretariat members.

Delegates leave with more than certificates. They leave with a drafted resolution they own, friendships across cities and schools, and the rare confidence of having defended a position under fire — politely, precisely and in perfect parliamentary order.

  • Authentic UNA-USA procedure — Formal rolls, moderated caucuses, amendment stacks — nothing is simulated halfway.

  • Champion executive boards — Every board has chaired or topped national circuits; feedback is written and personal.

  • Crisis injections & press influence — The International Press publishes daily verdicts that can move committee dynamics.

VIII About Us

The students who learn
to negotiate refuse
to escalate.

Who we are, why Hyderabad, and what the eighth edition of SOMUN is setting out to prove.

SOMUN is a student-run Model United Nations conference born in Hyderabad — built by delegates, for delegates, with no faculty hand on the wheel. Since our first gavel we have run on one belief: the habits you form across a committee table — listening hard, arguing fairly, signing only what you can defend — are the same habits that keep rooms calm long after the certificates yellow.

The MMXXVI edition convenes over three charged days, October 30 through November 1, 2026, at the Hyderabad International Convention Centre. Twelve chambers sit in session — from the Human Rights Council to continuous crisis — each catalogued as a gallery of dossiers and steered by an executive board of decorated delegates. An International Press corps publishes daily verdicts that travel far beyond the committee walls.

First-timer or gavel veteran, you get the same deal: authentic procedure, a dossier worth arguing over, and written feedback on every performance. Bring a position. Leave with a resolution you own — and a room that remembers your name.

  • October 30 — November 1, 2026 — Three days, one venue: Hyderabad International Convention Centre.

  • Twelve chambers — General assemblies, councils, crises and the Indian political theatre, one on stage at a time.

  • Run by delegates — Chairs, directors and the press corps are students who sat where you will sit, and review every delegate in writing.
